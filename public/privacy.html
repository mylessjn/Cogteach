Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

The short version

  • Cogteach turns one lesson from a teacher into a study set for each age group: ages 3–6, 7–11, 12–18 and 19+.
  • Our partner k-ID checks your age and works out your country, so the laws where you live decide when a parent must say yes and which features are on.
  • We keep your age group and country code, never your exact age or birthday.
  • Students never type to an AI. We don't sell your information and we don't show ads.
  • Parents and guardians can review, change or delete their child's information.

2. Information we collect

Your account

Teachers and students log in with a username and password. We store the username, the first name you choose to show, whether you are a teacher or a student, and your password in hashed form (we can't read it). We don't ask for your email address to make an account.

Age, country and permissions (through k-ID)

After you make an account, an age check run by our partner k-ID asks your age (or date of birth, depending on what your country allows) and works out your country or region from your internet connection. It then applies that place's laws. If a parent or guardian must approve, k-ID asks for their email address and sends them the request. Teachers also prove to k-ID that they are an adult, by email. k-ID handles this information under its own privacy policy.

From k-ID, our server receives the result of the age check. From it we keep only your age group (3–6, 7–11, 12–18 or 19+) and your country code (for example, "US"), plus the identifier of your k-ID session and which features k-ID allows for you. We do not store your exact age or date of birth.

Your class and learning progress

  • The class you join, and whether you are active or waiting for a parent.
  • Your progress: points, the days you studied (for streaks), your score and time on each lesson, and when flashcards are due again.
  • For ages 12–18, only if k-ID allows them for you: the avatar you pick, and your place on the class leaderboard.
  • Flags you send your teacher on a part of a lesson (a reason such as "I'm confused"), and an optional short note if k-ID allows private messages for you.

Your voice (microphone)

If voice is on for you in k-ID, some activities let you say a word out loud. When you tap the microphone, a clip of about 2.5 seconds is sent to our partner Inworld AI to turn it into text, so we can check the word. We don't store your recording or the text; we only keep whether you got a star. If voice is off, the microphone isn't shown.

Information stored on your device

Your browser keeps your login for the current tab (in session storage) and your k-ID session and any pending parent-request IDs (in local storage), so you can pick up where you left off. They are not used for advertising or to track you on other sites.

Teachers' lessons

When a teacher writes a lesson prompt, it is sent to AI models (see section 5) to build the study sets, voice lines and pictures. A teacher reviews them before students can see them. These steps use the lesson, not information about students.

Technical information

Like most websites, our servers receive basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you connect. We use it to run and secure the service.

3. How we use information

  • To run Cogteach and show you the study set for your age group.
  • To follow the laws where you live on age, parental consent and features (through k-ID).
  • To show your teacher your class membership, progress and flags.
  • To check spoken words, only when voice is on.
  • To keep Cogteach safe, prevent misuse and fix problems.
  • To answer your questions and requests, and meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or build advertising profiles.

4. Children's privacy

Cogteach is made for children as well as adults, so we take extra care.

  • Your country's rules. k-ID works out where you are and applies its laws. Below the age set there (for example, under 13 in the United States), a parent or guardian must approve before the child can use Cogteach, and some features need a parent's yes on their own.
  • Features stay off until allowed. Joining a class, voice, private notes, the leaderboard and avatars only turn on when k-ID allows them for the child. If k-ID doesn't allow a feature at all, we hide it.
  • No AI chat for students. Students never type to an AI model. Everything a child sees was written for their age group and, for ages 3–6 and 7–11, checked by a k-ID-verified adult before publishing.
  • Parents stay in control. Parents and guardians can review what we hold about their child, change which features are allowed, or ask us to delete it, by emailing myles.sjn@gmail.com. Players can change permissions in Settings; ones a guardian manages ask a grown-up to approve.
  • Least data. No email, photo or exact age is needed from a child.

6. How long we keep information

We keep account and progress information while your account is active. To delete an account, email myles.sjn@gmail.com; we then delete your Cogteach data and ask k-ID to delete your age-check session. Microphone clips are not kept by us. We may keep a small amount of information longer if the law requires it.

  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information or your account;
  • stop or limit how we use your information;
  • withdraw consent you or your parent gave earlier.

Email myles.sjn@gmail.com to make a request. We may need to confirm your identity first. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.
