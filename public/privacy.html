2. Information we collect

Age and permission information (through k-ID)

When you first open Cogteach, an age check run by our partner k-ID asks for your age or date of birth and works out which country or region you are in from your internet connection. If you are a child, k-ID asks for a parent or guardian's email address so they can approve your account. k-ID collects and handles this information under its own privacy policy.

From k-ID, Cogteach receives a session identifier, your age group, your country or region, and which features you are allowed to use. We use this to show you the right features and to keep the right protections in place.

Information stored on your device

Cogteach stores a few identifiers in your browser's local storage: a random player ID, your k-ID session ID, and the ID of any pending parent request. These let you pick up where you left off. They are not used for advertising or to track you on other sites.

Information you choose to share

  • Research library uploads. If the research library is turned on for you, anything you upload (such as research topics, notes or files) is stored by us and can be seen by other Cogteach users.
  • Voice tutor. If the voice tutor is turned on for you, what you say to it is processed so the AI tutor can understand and answer you (see section 5).
  • Messages to us. If you email us, we keep the message and your email address so we can reply.

Learning activity

If personalized recommendations are turned on for you, we use which lessons you open and finish to suggest what to learn next.

Technical information

Like most websites, our servers receive basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you connect. We use it to run and secure the service.

3. How we use information

  • To run Cogteach and show you age-appropriate content and features.
  • To check age and get parental permission where the law requires it.
  • To personalize learning, only when that feature is turned on.
  • To keep Cogteach safe, prevent misuse and fix problems.
  • To answer your questions and requests.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or build advertising profiles.

4. Children's privacy

Cogteach is designed to be used by children as well as adults, so we take extra care.

  • Parental consent. Where the law requires it (for example, under 13 in the United States, or under the age of digital consent in the EU and UK), a parent or guardian must approve before we turn on features that collect or share more information. k-ID sends that request and records the answer.
  • Features stay off until allowed. Features such as the research library, the voice tutor and personalized recommendations only turn on when the child's age and their parent's choices allow it.
  • Parents stay in control. Parents and guardians can review the information we hold about their child, change which features are allowed, ask us to stop collecting it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing myles.sjn@gmail.com. Players can also change feature permissions in Cogteach's Settings; features a guardian manages ask a grown-up to approve.
  • Least data. We don't ask children for more information than an activity needs.
